# CFB on CN

**College Football on Cartoon Network: the 1992-2004 checkerboard era as a full TV presentation for College
Football 27.**

One `.fbmod` restyles the whole broadcast: the score bug, other-game scores, the pregame and halftime screens, the
loading screen, touchdown and field goal graphics, the fonts, and the side panels on ultrawide screens.

## What's in it

- **Score bug:** a black-and-white checker rail along the bottom, with team colours, ranks, records, timeouts,
  possession, the play clock and the FLAG tab. Under it, a mini field shows the ball, the line of scrimmage and
  the line to gain, with "BALL ON OWN 35 | L HASH" beside it.
- **Corner scores:** other games' scores in a box at the top right instead of the bottom ticker (dynasty games;
  Play Now has no ticker).
- **Pregame:** a "NOW: AWAY VS HOME" strip on the matchup screen.
- **Halftime:** the halftime page becomes a comic-book "Around the Toon-iverse" page.
- **Loading screen:** a rolling checkerboard NOW bumper, with Courage peeking in and waving.
- **Touchdowns:** a checkerboard TOUCHDOWN! strip with Johnny Bravo flexing. **Field goals:** a matching FIELD GOAL! strip.
- **Fonts:** chunky cartoon lettering on the bug, scores and banners.
- **Ultrawide:** checkerboard side panels on the menus instead of the team colour.

## Pick one file

| File | Score bug |
|---|---|
| `CFBonCN.fbmod` | checker rail, bottom centre |
| `CFBonCN-BugB.fbmod` | corner box, bottom right |

Enable only one of them.

## Install

1. In the Mod Manager, import the `.fbmod` you picked.
2. Turn off any other score bug or broadcast package (NBC, ESPN, CFP ESPN and similar), and any Hide Scorebug mod.
   They replace the same screens.
3. Apply and launch.
